Why this exists

Every garment sold in the US must carry care instructions (FTC Care Labeling Rule, 16 CFR Part 423), and most of the world uses the five-symbol ISO 3758 / GINETEX system. Nobody remembers what a triangle with two slanted lines means. WashCode decodes all of it: 33 symbols across washing, bleaching, tumble and natural drying, ironing, and professional care.

What you get

  • Tap-to-build your tag: pick the exact symbols printed on the label
  • Plain-English instructions in the standard order: wash, bleach, dry, iron, professional
  • Temperatures in °C and °F, cycle guidance (normal / permanent press / gentle)
  • Full reference chart for every symbol, plus search ("tumble", "gentle", "shade")
  • Works fully offline in your browser - nothing leaves your device

Grounded in the real standard

Symbol meanings follow ISO 3758:2012 (international, numbers are max °C) and ASTM D5489 (US dots variant), cross-checked against the FTC's published care-symbol chart and public references. A milder treatment than the label shows is always acceptable.

WashCode is a reference tool, not garment-specific advice - when in doubt, gentler is safer.
